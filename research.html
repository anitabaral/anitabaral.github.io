Enhancing ALPR Systems

The purpose of the project was to assess the use of SRGAN (Super-resolution Generative Adversarial Network) to enhance the performance of the ALPR (Automatic License Plate Recognition) systems. The research done for the project has been published in Springer.

Audio Generation With Embedded Video Context

The research explores the domain of audio generation for videos, with a particular emphasis on introducing conditionality to the process. The focus of the research lies in the development of a model capable of synchronizing audio waveforms with video inputs, thereby engaging in conditional audio generation. The research seeks techniques to reduce complexities during training and alignment of the two domains, particularly in the sensitive time domain. Motivated by the potential to enhance audio in video content, the research aims to remove unwanted noise, align audio seamlessly, and improve the overall performance of audio-based applications.

RARA: Road-marking Automation

Reprogrammable Automatic Road Aligner (RARA) was built to mark roads, courts, and parking using Thermoplastic Paint, via a reprogrammable interface. Automating the manual work of road-marking using a rover, run via an Android app.
