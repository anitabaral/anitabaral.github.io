# projects

Myaamia text to speech

Neural TTS for an Indigenous language, comparing FastSpeech, Glow-TTS and VITS. VITS gave the best spectral and prosodic accuracy.

PyTorch · VITS · wandb

Audio from video context

A generative model using a state space model conditioned on image spectrograms to produce long, context-aware audio.

PyTorch · SSM · diffusion

Nepali license plate recognition

Detects and reads Nepali plates with WPOD-NET, contour segmentation and SVMs at 90% accuracy, trained on a dataset the team built.

TensorFlow · OpenCV · source

Single image super resolution

An SRGAN trained on license plate images to sharpen distorted plates before recognition, improving accuracy by 40%.

TensorFlow · GAN · source
